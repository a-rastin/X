Pregnancy & Lactation

Pregnancy

There are no randomized clinical studies of use during pregnancy

Available data from published epidemiological studies and postmarketing case reports
of pregnancy outcomes following inhaled albuterol use do not consistently demonstrate
a risk of major birth defects or miscarriage

In animal reproduction studies, when administered SC to pregnant mice there was
evidence of cleft palate at ≤9x the maximum recommended human daily inhalation dose

Clinical considerations

Disease-associated maternal and/or embryo/fetal risk
In women with poorly or moderately controlled asthma, there is an increased
risk of preeclampsia in the mother and prematurity, low birth weight, and
small for gestational age in the neonate

There are no available data on the presence of albuterol in human milk, the effects on

Consider developmental and health benefits of breastfeeding along with mother’s

B: May be acceptable. Either animal studies show no risk but human studies not available

Oral Inhalation Administration

Aerosol metered-dose inhaler (MDI)

Prime inhaler (before first-time use or when inhaler has not been used for >2
weeks): Release 4 test sprays into the air, away from the face

Shake well before each use

Breathe out fully through the mouth; place mouthpiece fully into mouth, holding

the metal canister with your index finger

Hold your breath for ≤10 sec before breathing out

For additional puffs, wait 1 minute, shake inhaler again, and repeat steps listed
above; replace cap after use

Cleaning: To maintain product integrity, wash and dry mouthpiece thoroughly at
least 1x/week
