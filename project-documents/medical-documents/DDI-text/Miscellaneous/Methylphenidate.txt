ventricular arrhythmia)

dependence while on therapy

Limitations of use (Aptensio XR)

Not recommended in pediatric patients <6 years of age; patients <6 years of age
experienced higher plasma exposure than patients aged ≥6 at the same dose and
high rates of adverse reactions, most notably weight loss

Pregnancy

Published studies and postmarketing reports on use during pregnancy have not

or fetal outcomes

Animal data

No teratogenic effects were observed with oral administration of methylphenidate

adolescents

CNS stimulant medications can cause vasoconstriction and thereby decrease
