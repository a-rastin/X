Dosing Considerations

Use only to treat infections that are proven or strongly suspected to be caused by
susceptible bacteria to reduce the development of drug-resistant bacteria and maintain
the effectiveness of azithromycin

Limitations of use

Do not use in patients with pneumonia who may be inappropriate for oral
therapy because of moderate to severe illness or risk factors such as any
of the following:

Patients with cystic fibrosis

Patients with nosocomial infections

Patients with known or suspected bacteremia

Patients requiring hospitalization

Elderly or debilitated patients

Patients with significant underlying health problems that may compromise
their ability to respond to their illness (including immunodeficiency or
functional asplenia)

Tablets or oral suspension (1 g/5 mL): Store at <30ºC (86ºF)

Unopened vial: Store at <30ºC (86ºF)

Reconstituted vial: Store at <30ºC (86ºF) for 24 hr

Diluted solutions: Store <30ºC (86ºF) for 24 hr OR refrigerate at 5ºC (41ºF)

Reconstituted oral suspension: Store at 5-30ºC (41-86ºF) and use within 10 days;
discard after full dosing is completed
