Mechanism of Action

Inhibits transmembrane influx of extracellular calcium ions across membranes of
myocardial cells and vascular smooth muscle cells without changing serum calcium
concentrations; this inhibits cardiac and vascular smooth muscle contraction, thereby
dilating main coronary and systemic arteries

Increases myocardial oxygen delivery in patients with vasospastic angina

Absorption

Bioavailability: 64-90%

Onset: 24-96 hr

Duration: 24 hr (antihypertensive effect)

Peak plasma time: 6-12 hr

Steady-state plasma levels are reached after 7-8 days of consecutive dosing

Distribution

Protein bound: 93-98%

Vd: 21 L/kg
