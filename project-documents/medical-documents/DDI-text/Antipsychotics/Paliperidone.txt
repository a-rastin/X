Schizophrenia
PO
• 6 mg PO qAM; may be titrated upward or downward in increments of 3 mg/day at intervals >= 5 days; not to exceed 12 mg/day

IM, extended-released 1-month (Invega Sustenna)
• 234 mg in deltoid on treatment day 1, then 156 mg 1 week later (day 8)
• Recommended maintenance dose is 117 mg IM once monthly, although some patients may require lower or higher dosages (monthly dose range 39-234 mg)

IM, extended-release 1-month (Erzofri)
• Initial dose (Day 1): 351 mg IM in deltoid muscle; administer monthly dose 4 weeks after first injection
• Monthly dose: 39-234 mg IM in deltoid or gluteal muscle
• Recommended monthly dose for treatment of schizophrenia is 117 mg; some patients may benefit from lower or higher monthly doses within the additional available strengths (39 mg, 78 mg, 156 mg, and 234 mg)
• Switching from long-acting injectable antipsychotics:
  - Initiate therapy in place of next scheduled injection as described above
  - Continue at monthly intervals

IM, extended-released 3-months (Invega Trinza)
• Patients must be adequately treated with Invega Sustenna (1-month paliperidone) for at least 4 months before initiating Invega Trinza
• Dose initiation dependent on once-monthly Invega Sustenna dose: Initiate Invega Trinza when the next 1-month paliperidone dose is scheduled with an Invega Trinza dose based on the previous 1-month injection dose

Conversion from monthly injection to 3-month injection
• Invega Sustenna 78 mg/month: Initiate Invega Trinza at 273 mg IM q3mo
• Invega Sustenna 117 mg/month: Initiate Invega Trinza at 410 mg IM q3mo
• Invega Sustenna 156 mg/month: Initiate Invega Trinza at 546 mg IM q3mo
• Invega Sustenna 234 mg/month: Initiate Invega Trinza at 819 mg IM q3mo

Conversion from 3-month IM injection to extended-release tablets
• 273 mg IM (last 3 months to 24wk): 3 mg ER tab
• 410 mg IM (last 3 months to 24wk): 3 mg ER tab; 6 mg if >24wk
• 546 mg IM (last 3 months to 18 wk): 3 mg ER tab; 6 mg if 18-24 wk; 9 mg if >24 wk
• 819 mg IM (last 3 months to 18 wk): 6 mg ER tab; 9 mg if 18-24 wk; 12 mg if >24 wk

IM, extended-released 6-months (Invega Hafyera)
• Patients must be adequately treated with Invega Sustenna (1-month paliperidone) for at least 4 months OR with Invega Trinza (3-month paliperidone) for at least one 3-month cycle before initiating Invega Hafyera

Conversion from monthly injection to 6-month injection
• Initiate Invega Hafyera when next Invega Sustenna dose is scheduled
• Invega Sustenna 156 mg/month: Initiate Invega Hafyera at 1,092 mg IM q6mo
• Invega Sustenna 234 mg/month: Initiate Invega Hafyera at 1,560 mg IM q6mo
• There are no equivalent doses of Invega Hafyera for Invega Sustenna 39-mg, 78-mg, or 117-mg doses, which were not studied

Conversion from 3-month injection to 6-month injection
• Initial Invega Hafyera dose is based on the previous Invega Trinza
• Initiate Invega Hafyera when next Invega Trinza dose is scheduled
• May administer Invega Hafyera dose up to 2 weeks before or after the next scheduled Invega Trinza
• Invega Trinza 546 mg q3mo: Initiate Invega Hafyera at 1,092 mg IM q6mo
• Invega Trinza 819 mg q3mo: Initiate Invega Hafyera at 1,560 mg IM q6mo
• There are no equivalent doses of Invega Hafyera for Invega Trinza 273-mg or 410-mg doses, which were not studied

Dosing interval and dosage adjustments
• Following initial dose, administer Invega Hafyera IM q6mo
• May adjust dosage every 6 months between 1,092 mg and 1,560 mg based on individual response and tolerability, if necessary
• Owing to the potential longer duration to Invega Hafyera, patient's response to an adjusted dose may not be apparent for several months

Schizoaffective Disorder
• Indicated for schizoaffective disorder as monotherapy and as an adjunct to mood stabilizers or antidepressants
• 6 mg PO qDay in am (range 3-12 mg); titration may not be necessary; if exceeding 6 mg/day, increases of 3 mg/day recommended at intervals of 4 days of more; not to exceed 12 mg/day

IM, extended-released 1-month (Invega Sustenna)
• IM (initial): 234 mg in deltoid on treatment day 1, then 156 mg 1 week later (day 8)
• IM (maintenance): Administer once each month IM in deltoid or gluteal muscle; adjust dose based on tolerability and/or efficacy using available strengths; maintenance dose ranges between 78-234 mg once monthly

IM, extended-release 1-month (Erzofri)
• May give injection up to 7 days before or after monthly time point
• Initial dose (Day 1): 351 mg IM in deltoid muscle; administer monthly dose 4 weeks after first injection
• Monthly dose: 78-234 mg IM in deltoid or gluteal muscle
• Recommended monthly dose for treatment of schizophrenia is 117 mg; some patients may benefit from lower or higher monthly doses within the additional available strengths (78 mg, 156 mg, and 234 mg)
• Adjust dose based on tolerability and/or efficacy using available strengths; the 39-mg strength was not studied in the long-term schizoaffective disorder study

Dosage Modifications

Renal impairment (PO)
• CrCl 50-79 mL/min: 3 mg/day initially; not to exceed 6 mg/day
• CrCl 10-49 mL/min: 1.5 mg/day initially; not to exceed 3 mg/day
• CrCl < 10 ml/min: Not recommended

Renal impairment (IM)
• Invega Hafyera: Not recommended for all severities of renal impairment
• Invega Sustenna:
  - For patients with mild renal impairment (creatinine clearance >= 50 mL/min to < 80 mL/min), initiate Invega Sustenna with a dose of 156 mg on treatment Day 1 and 117 mg on Day 8, both in the deltoid muscle; follow with recommended monthly maintenance dose of 78 mg, administered in either the deltoid or gluteal muscle; adjust monthly maintenance dose based on tolerability and/or efficacy within strengths of 39 mg, 78 mg, 117 mg, or 156 mg; maximum monthly dose is 156 mg for patients with mild renal impairment
  - Moderate-to-severe (CrCl < 50 mL/min): Not recommended
• Erzofri:
  - Mild (CrCl 50-79 mL/min): 234 mg in deltoid on treatment day 1, then 78 mg monthly maintenance; adjust dose as needed, not to exceed 156 mg/month
  - Moderate-to-severe (CrCl < 50 mL/min): Not recommended
• Invega Trinza, Invega Hafyera:
  - This drug has not been systematically studied in patients with renal impairment; for patients with mild renal impairment (creatinine clearance >= 50 mL/min to < 80 mL/min), adjust dosage and stabilize the patient using PP1M before transitioning from PP1M to paliperidone palmitate, or from PP1M to PP3M before transitioning to paliperidone palmitate; refer to prescribing information of PP1M or PP3M product for recommended PP1M or PP3M dosage in patients with mild renal impairment
  - Moderate to severe (CrCl < 50 mL/min): Not recommended

Hepatic impairment
• IM
  - Mild-to-moderate: Based on a study with oral paliperidone, no dose adjustment required
  - Severe: Not studied
• PO
  - Mild-to-moderate: No dosage adjustment necessary
  - Severe: Not studied

Dosing Considerations
• Patients with Parkinson disease or dementia with Lewy bodies can experience increased sensitivity to paliperidone. Manifestations can include confusion, obtundation, postural instability with frequent falls, extrapyramidal symptoms, and clinical features consistent with neuroleptic malignant syndrome.

Postmarketing Reports
Angioedema, catatonia, ileus, somnambulism, swollen tongue, thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura, urinary incontinence, urinary retention. Anaphylactic reactions (Invega Sustenna).

WARNINGS

Black Box Warnings
Increased mortality in elderly patients with dementia-related psychosis: Elderly patients treated with antipsychotic drugs are at an increased risk of death. Not approved for use in patients with dementia-related psychosis.

Contraindications
Documented hypersensitivity to paliperidone or risperidone.

Cautions
• Cerebrovascular adverse reactions (e.g., stroke, transient ischemic attack) reported.
• Neuroleptic malignant syndrome (NMS) reported; discontinue treatment if suspected.
• May induce orthostatic hypotension; use with caution in patients with cardiovascular/cerebrovascular disease.
• Motor instability, somnolence, and orthostatic hypotension may lead to falls/fractures.
• May cause CNS depression; impairs ability to operate heavy machinery.
• Use cautiously in patients with a history of seizures.
• Associated with esophageal dysmotility and aspiration.
• Rare cases of priapism reported.
• Caution with core body temperature elevations (e.g., extreme heat, exercise, dehydration).
• Avoid in severe preexisting GI stenosis.
• Rare cases of intraoperative floppy iris syndrome (IFIS) with risperidone.
• Patients with Parkinson disease may be more sensitive to effects.
• May mask toxicity of other drugs/conditions due to antiemetic effects.
• Caution in patients with suicidal ideation or psychotic illness.
• Risk of Torsades de pointes with QTc-prolonging drugs, hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia, or bradycardia.
• Tardive dyskinesia risk increases with duration and cumulative dose.
• Metabolic changes: Hyperglycemia, diabetes mellitus (sometimes fatal), hyperprolactinemia, dyslipidemia, weight gain.
• Leukopenia, neutropenia, and agranulocytosis reported.

Drug interaction overview
• Substrate of P-gp and CYP3A4.
• Avoid strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers with Invega Hafyera.
• Paliperidone may antagonize levodopa/dopamine agonists.

PREGNANCY & LACTATION

Pregnancy
• Neonates exposed during the third trimester are at risk for extrapyramidal/withdrawal symptoms.
• Available data have not established a drug-associated risk for major birth defects.
• Drug detected up to 18 months after a single 3-month injection.
• Pregnancy exposure registry available.

Lactation
• Paliperidone is present in human breast milk.
• Monitor infants for sedation, failure to thrive, jitteriness, and extrapyramidal symptoms.

Pregnancy Categories
Not strictly defined (NA), but standard precautions for antipsychotics apply.

PHARMACOLOGY

Mechanism of Action
• Paliperidone palmitate is hydrolyzed to paliperidone, a major active metabolite of risperidone.
• Efficacy mediated through a combination of central dopamine D2 and serotonin 5HT2A receptor antagonism.

Absorption
• Bioavailability: 28%
• Peak plasma time: PO (24 hr), Invega Sustenna (13 days), Invega Trinza (30-33 days), Invega Hafyera (29-32 days).

Distribution
• Protein bound: 74%
• Vd: PO (487 L), Invega Sustenna (391 L), Invega Trinza/Hafyera (1,960 L).

Metabolism & Elimination
• Metabolized by CYP2D6 and CYP3A4.
• Excretion (PO): Urine (80%), feces (11%).
• Half-Life: PO (23 hr), Invega Sustenna (25-49 days), Invega Trinza (118-139 days), Invega Hafyera (148-159 days).

ADMINISTRATION

Oral Administration
• Take with or without food.
• Swallow whole; do not chew, crush, or divide.
• Tablet shell is eliminated in stool.
• Avoid alcohol.

IM Preparation
• Shake prefilled syringe vigorously for at least 15 seconds within 5 minutes before administration. Invega Hafyera must be shaken VERY FAST.

IM Administration
• Administer only by a healthcare professional.
• Inspect for foreign matter. Do not mix with other products.
• Avoid inadvertent blood vessel injection. Administer as a single injection.
• Inject slowly, deep into upper-outer gluteal muscle (or deltoid depending on the phase/product). Alternate gluteal muscles.

Missed Doses
Detailed guidelines are provided for missed doses spanning <4 weeks to >11 months depending on the specific injectable formulation (Sustenna, Erzofri, Trinza, Hafyera), generally requiring re-initiation doses if significant time has elapsed.

STORAGE

Tablets
• Store up to 25°C (77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
• Protect from moisture. Keep out of reach of children.

IM
• Store at room temperature 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions permitted to 15-30°C (59-86°F).
• Do not mix with any other product or diluent.
• Ship and store in a horizontal position (see carton arrows).
