================================================================================
ADULT DOSING & USES [cite: 8, 9]
================================================================================
Schizophrenia [cite: 42]
---------------------
* PO: 10-15 mg/day PO initially; may increase after 2 weeks; not to exceed 30 mg/day PO for tablets; efficacy not significantly greater above 15 mg/day[cite: 46, 47].
* Abilify Maintena: Establish tolerability with PO first (may take up to ~2 weeks)[cite: 51, 52]. Recommended dose is 400 mg monthly (no sooner than 26 days after previous injection)[cite: 50]. 
  - 1-day initiation: Two IM injections of 400 mg in separate sites, plus one dose of oral aripiprazole 20 mg on day 1[cite: 53, 54, 55].
  - 14-day initiation: One IM injection of 400 mg, plus continue oral aripiprazole (10 mg to 20 mg) or other oral antipsychotics for 14 consecutive days[cite: 56, 57, 58, 64, 65].
  - Adverse reactions: May reduce dosage to 300 mg once monthly[cite: 66].
* Aristada (IM once monthly): Establish tolerability with PO first[cite: 68]. Base initial dose on current PO dose; coadminister PO for 21 consecutive days[cite: 69]. 
  - 10 mg/day PO: 441 mg IM once monthly[cite: 70].
  - 15 mg/day PO: 662 mg IM once monthly, 882 mg IM q6wk, or 1064 mg IM q2mo[cite: 71].
  - >=20 mg/day PO: 882 mg IM once monthly[cite: 71].
  - Early dosing: Do not give earlier than 14 days after the previous injection[cite: 73].
* Aristada Initio (single IM dose): Indicated in combination with oral aripiprazole for initiation or re-initiation after a missed dose of Aristada[cite: 75, 76]. Administered as a single 675 mg IM dose in conjunction with the first Aristada IM injection and one dose of aripiprazole 30 mg PO[cite: 79, 80].
* Abilify Asimtufii (IM every 2 months): Establish tolerability with oral first[cite: 83, 84]. Recommended dose is 960 mg IM q2Months[cite: 85].
  - 1-day initiation: 960 mg Abilify Asimtufii IM in gluteal muscle, one injection of Abilify Maintena 400 mg in separate muscle, and one oral 20 mg dose on day 1[cite: 93, 94, 95].
  - 14-day initiation: 960 mg IM in gluteal muscle, plus continue oral aripiprazole (10-20 mg) or other oral antipsychotics for 14 consecutive days[cite: 96, 97].
  - Switching from Abilify Maintena: Administer 960 mg once q2months in place of next scheduled injection (second or later injection of Abilify Maintena)[cite: 98, 99, 100].
  - Adverse reactions: May reduce dosage to 720 mg once q2months[cite: 101]. Window allowed up to 2 weeks before or after scheduled timepoint[cite: 102].

Bipolar Mania [cite: 103]
-------------
* PO: Indicated for acute and maintenance treatment of manic or mixed episodes as monotherapy or adjunct to lithium or valproate[cite: 106, 107].
  - Monotherapy: 15 mg/day PO initially; may increase gradually; not to exceed 30 mg/day[cite: 108].
  - Adjunct: 10-15 mg/day PO initially; recommended target is 15 mg/day; not to exceed 30 mg/day[cite: 109, 110]. Continue stabilization dose up to 6 weeks[cite: 111].
* Abilify Maintena: Establish tolerability with oral first[cite: 120]. Administer 400 mg IM once monthly (no sooner than 26 days apart)[cite: 118, 124, 125]. Continue oral aripiprazole (10-20 mg/day) or other oral antipsychotics for 14 consecutive days after initial injection[cite: 122]. Consider reduction to 300 mg/month if adverse reactions occur[cite: 126].
* Abilify Asimtufii (IM every 2 months): 960 mg IM q2Months[cite: 127, 130]. Requires 14 days of oral aripiprazole (10-20 mg/day) or alternative oral antipsychotic stabilization[cite: 132, 135, 136]. Can transition directly from Abilify Maintena[cite: 137, 138, 139]. Dose reduction to 720 mg q2months allowed for adverse reactions[cite: 140].

Major Depressive Disorder [cite: 147]
-------------------------
* PO: Used adjunctively with other antidepressants[cite: 149]. 2-5 mg/day PO initially; increased weekly PRN by <=5 mg/day to a range of 2-15 mg/day[cite: 148].

Orphan Designation [cite: 215]
------------------
* N-Glycanase 1 Deficiency: Orphan designation for treatment of NGLY1 deficiency[cite: 216].

================================================================================
PEDIATRIC DOSING & USES [cite: 219]
================================================================================
Schizophrenia [cite: 242]
-------------
* <13 years: Safety and efficacy not established[cite: 244].
* 13-17 years: 2 mg/day PO initially; increase to 5 mg/day after 2 days; may increase to target dose of 10 mg/day after additional 2 days[cite: 245, 246, 247]. Subsequent increments can be 5 mg/day[cite: 248]. 30 mg/day was not shown to be more efficacious than 10 mg/day[cite: 249].

Bipolar Mania [cite: 250]
-------------
* 10-17 years: Initiate at 2 mg/day PO; increase to 5 mg/day after 2 days; may increase to target of 10 mg/day after additional 2 days[cite: 252, 253, 254]. Subsequent doses may increase by 5 mg/day[cite: 255]. Maintenance: 10-30 mg/day[cite: 256].

Autism [cite: 257]
------
* <6 years: Safety and efficacy not established[cite: 259].
* 6-17 years: 2 mg/day PO initially; increase gradually at >=1 week intervals to target dosage of 5 mg/day; may increase PRN to 10 mg/day or higher; not to exceed 15 mg/day[cite: 260, 261].

Tourette Disorder [cite: 262]
-----------------
* <6 years: Safety and efficacy not established[cite: 264].
* 6-18 years (<50 kg): Initiate at 2 mg/day PO; target dose 5 mg/day after 2 days[cite: 265, 266]. Can increase to 10 mg/day if optimal control is not achieved[cite: 271]. Adjustments occur at intervals of no less than 1 week[cite: 272].
* 6-18 years (>=50 kg): Initiate at 2 mg/day PO for 2 days, then 5 mg/day for 5 days, targeting 10 mg/day on day 8[cite: 273, 274]. Can increase up to 20 mg/day if needed[cite: 275]. Adjustments occur in 5 mg/day increments at intervals of no less than 1 week[cite: 276].

================================================================================
DOSAGE MODIFICATIONS [cite: 150, 158, 169, 196]
================================================================================
Oral Formulations (Adult & Pediatric) [cite: 150, 277]
-------------------------------------
* Known CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers: Administer half of recommended dose[cite: 152, 279].
* Known CYP2D6 Poor Metabolizers taking concomitant strong CYP3A4 inhibitors: Administer a quarter of the recommended dose (decrease dose by 75%)[cite: 153, 154, 280, 281].
* Strong CYP2D6 or CYP3A4 inhibitors: Administer half of recommended dose[cite: 155, 282].
* Strong CYP2D6 AND CYP3A4 inhibitors: Administer a quarter of the recommended dose (decrease dose by 75%)[cite: 156, 283].
* Strong CYP3A4 inducers: Double recommended dose over 1-2 weeks[cite: 157, 284].

Abilify Maintena [cite: 158]
----------------
* CYP2D6 poor metabolizers: 300 mg IM[cite: 159].
* CYP2D6 poor metabolizers taking concomitant CYP3A4 inhibitor: 200 mg IM[cite: 160].
* Patients taking 400 mg IM: 
  - Strong CYP2D6 OR CYP3A4 inhibitors: 300 mg IM[cite: 161, 162].
  - CYP2D6 AND CYP3A4 inhibitors: 200 mg IM[cite: 163].
  - CYP3A4 inducers: Avoid use[cite: 164].
* Patients taking 300 mg IM:
  - Strong CYP2D6 OR CYP3A4 inhibitors: 200 mg IM[cite: 165, 166].
  - CYP2D6 AND CYP3A4 inhibitors: 160 mg IM[cite: 167].
  - CYP3A4 inducers: Avoid use[cite: 168].

Aristada [cite: 169]
--------
* Modulators added for <2 weeks: No dosage changes[cite: 170].
* Strong CYP3A4 inhibitor for >2 weeks: Reduce dose to next lower strength; no adjustment needed for 441 mg if tolerated[cite: 171, 172].
* Poor CYP2D6 metabolizers: Reduce dose to 441 mg from higher strengths; no adjustment needed if already taking 441 mg[cite: 177, 178].
* Strong CYP2D6 inhibitor for >2 weeks: Reduce dose to next lower strength; no adjustment needed for 441 mg if tolerated[cite: 179, 180, 181]. Poor CYP2D6 metabolizers require no further dose adjustment[cite: 182].
* Both strong CYP3A4 and CYP2D6 inhibitors for >2 weeks: Avoid use for higher strengths; no adjustment needed for 441 mg if tolerated[cite: 183, 184, 185].
* CYP3A4 inducers for >2 weeks: No adjustment for higher strengths; increase 441 mg dose to 662 mg[cite: 186, 187, 188].

Aristada Initio [cite: 189]
---------------
* No dosage adjustments possible (single strength/single-dose syringe)[cite: 190].
* CYP2D6 poor metabolizers, strong CYP3A4 inhibitors, and strong CYP3A4 inducers: Avoid use[cite: 191].

Abilify Asimtufii [cite: 196]
-----------------
* CYP2D6 poor metabolizers: 720 mg q2months[cite: 197, 198]. If taking concomitant CYP3A4 inhibitors: Avoid use[cite: 199].
* Coadministration with strong CYP2D6 OR strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (960 mg dose): Decrease to 720 mg q2months[cite: 200, 201, 202, 203].
* Coadministration with strong CYP2D6 AND strong CYP3A4 inhibitors: Avoid use[cite: 208, 209].
* Coadministration with strong CYP3A4 inducers: Avoid use[cite: 210, 211].

Hepatic & Renal Impairment [cite: 192, 194]
--------------------------
* Hepatic (Child-Pugh score 5-15): Mild-to-severe impairment requires no dosage adjustments[cite: 193].
* Renal (GFR 15-90 mL/min): Mild-to-severe impairment requires no dosage adjustments[cite: 195].

================================================================================
WARNINGS & CAUTIONS [cite: 2197]
================================================================================
Black Box Warnings [cite: 2198]
------------------
* Dementia-related Psychosis: Not approved for dementia-related psychosis; patients treated with antipsychotic drugs are at increased risk of death (appeared cardiovascular or infectious in nature)[cite: 2200, 2201].
* Suicidal thoughts and behaviors (Aripiprazole PO and Abilify injection only): Antidepressants increased risk of suicidal thinking/behavior in children, adolescents, and young adults (<24 years); monitor closely[cite: 2203, 2204, 2205].

Cautions [cite: 2212]
--------
* Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS): Monitor for fever, muscle rigidity, altered mental status, autonomic instability[cite: 2215].
* Tardive Dyskinesia & EPS: Consider discontinuation if clinically indicated[cite: 2213, 2214].
* Falls Assessment: Somnolence, postural hypotension, motor instability can lead to fractures/injuries; complete recurrent fall risk assessments[cite: 2216, 2217].
* Cardiovascular / Cerebrovascular Disease: Use caution; may increase stroke, TIA, or fatalities; monitor heart rate/blood pressure[cite: 2218, 2219, 2220].
* Seizures: Lowered seizure thresholds may occur (especially >=65 years)[cite: 2224, 2225].
* Cognitive/Motor Impairment: Caution when operating hazardous machinery or automobiles[cite: 2226, 2229, 2230].
* Metabolic Changes: Associated with dyslipidemia, significant weight gain, and hyperglycemia. Hyperglycemia can cause ketoacidosis, hyperosmolar coma, or death. Monitor glucose regularly[cite: 2247, 2248, 2249, 2251, 2252].
* Leukopenia, Neutropenia, Agranulocytosis: If patient has history of significant low WBC, monitor CBC frequently. Discontinue if WBC declines <1000/mcL in absence of other causes[cite: 2243, 2244, 2245, 2246].
* Errors: Avoid substitution errors between Aristada and Aristada Initio[cite: 2238, 2240, 2242].

================================================================================
PREGNANCY & LACTATION [cite: 2265]
================================================================================
Pregnancy [cite: 2266]
---------
* Registry: National Pregnancy Registry for Atypical Antipsychotics (1-866-961-2388)[cite: 2268].
* Fetal Risks: Neonates exposed during third trimester are at risk for extrapyramidal and/or withdrawal symptoms (agitation, hypertonia, hypotonia, tremor, somnolence, respiratory distress, feeding disorder)[cite: 2270, 2273].
* Maternal Risks: Untreated schizophrenia risks include relapse, hospitalization, suicide, and preterm birth[cite: 2278, 2279]. Drug exposure may decrease milk supply in postpartum period[cite: 2277].

Lactation [cite: 2281]
---------
* Excretion: Aripiprazole is present in human breast milk[cite: 2282]. 
* Milk Supply: May lead to clinically relevant decreases or total absence of maternal milk supply (mediated through decreased prolactin levels)[cite: 2282, 2283, 2284].
* Infant Monitoring: Monitor the breastfed infant for dehydration and lack of appropriate weight gain[cite: 2285].

================================================================================
PHARMACOLOGY [cite: 2298]
================================================================================
* Mechanism of Action: Atypical antipsychotic; partial agonist at dopamine D2 and serotonin type 1 (5-HT1A) receptors; antagonist at serotonin type 2 (5-HT2A) receptor; possesses alpha-blocking activity[cite: 2300, 2301].
* Bioavailability: 87% (tablet); 100% (IM)[cite: 2305].
* Peak Plasma Time: 1-3 hr (IR); 3-5 hr (tablet); 5-7 hr (ER); ~27 days (Aristada Initio)[cite: 2306].
* Distribution: Protein bound 99%[cite: 2308]. Vd: 404 L (aripiprazole IV); 268 L (Aristada Initio)[cite: 2309].
* Metabolism: Hepatic via CYP2D6 and CYP3A4[cite: 2310, 2311]. Major active metabolite is Dehydroaripiprazole (40%)[cite: 2312].
* Elimination Half-life: 75 hr (parent drug); 94 hr (metabolite); 30-47 days (IM); 146 hr (poor metabolizers)[cite: 2318]. Aristada Initio half-life: 15-18 days[cite: 2319].
* Excretion: Feces (55%), urine (25%)[cite: 2320].

================================================================================
ADMINISTRATION & STORAGE
================================================================================
Oral Administration [cite: 2322]
-------------------
* Oral Solution: Substitution on mg-per-mg basis up to 25 mg level; a 30 mg tablet patient should receive 25 mg of solution[cite: 2323, 2324].
* Tablets / ODT / Film: May be taken with or without food[cite: 2326, 2329, 2335].
* Tablets: Swallow whole; do not divide, crush, or chew[cite: 2327].
* Oral Film: Apply on top of tongue to dissolve. Do not chew, swallow undissolved, cut, or split[cite: 2330, 2331]. Administer only 1 film at a time[cite: 2332].
* ODT: Open only when ready. Place on tongue with dry hands[cite: 2336, 2339]. Rapidly disintegrates; recommended to take without liquid, but permissible[cite: 2340]. Do not push through foil or split[cite: 2338, 2341].

IM Preparation & Administration [cite: 2342, 2371, 2385, 2398]
--------------------------------
* Universal IM rule: Must be administered by a healthcare professional deep IM; do not give IV or SC[cite: 119, 2399, 2400]. Do not massage injection site[cite: 2403].
* Abilify Maintena Lyophilized Vial: Reconstitute with Sterile Water for Injection (SWI)[cite: 2349]. 400mg vial takes 1.9 mL SWI; 300mg vial takes 1.5 mL SWI (Final concentration 200 mg/mL)[cite: 2350, 2351, 2352]. Shake vigorously for 30 seconds[cite: 2353]. Inject immediately[cite: 2355].
* Abilify Maintena Prefilled Syringe: Rotate plunger rod to release diluent; shake vigorously for 20 seconds[cite: 2360, 2361]. Inject immediately[cite: 2363].
* Aristada & Aristada Initio Syringes: Tap syringe at least 10 times to dislodge settled material; shake vigorously for minimum 30 seconds before use[cite: 2372, 2373, 2386, 2387]. -------

* Oral Film: 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions 15-30°C (59-86°F)[cite: 2442].
* Abilify Maintena Prefilled Syringe: Store below 30°C (86°F). Do not freeze. Protect from light[cite: 2443, 2444].
* Aristada & Aristada Initio: Store at room temperature 25°C (77°F) or 20-25°C (68-77°F); excursions 15-30°C (59-86°F). Do not freeze[cite: 2446, 2447].
