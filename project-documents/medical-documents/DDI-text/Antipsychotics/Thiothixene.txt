WARNINGS [cite: 1304]
Black Box Warnings [cite: 1305]
* Patients with dementia-related psychosis who are treated with antipsychotic drugs are at an increased risk of death as shown in short-term controlled trials. [cite: 1306]
* The deaths appeared to be either cardiovascular (eg, heart failure, sudden death) or infectious (eg, pneumonia) in nature. [cite: 1307]
* This drug is not approved for the treatment of patients with dementia-related psychosis. [cite: 1308]

Contraindications [cite: 1309]
* Documented hypersensitivity, Blood dyscrasias, Coma, Circulatory collapse, CNS depression (including coma) [cite: 1314, 1315, 1316, 1317, 1318]

Cautions [cite: 1319]
* Potential cross-sensitivity between thioxanthenes & phenothiazine derivative [cite: 1320]
* Not for use for dementia in elderly [cite: 1321]
* Antiemetic effect may mask signs of overdosage of toxic drugs and may obscure conditions such as intestinal obstruction and brain tumor [cite: 1322]
* Extreme caution in patients with history of convulsive disorders or alcohol withdrawal; may lower convulsive threshold [cite: 1323, 1324]
* Exhibits weak anticholinergic properties; caution in extreme heat or with atropine/related drugs [cite: 1325]
* Use with caution in cardiovascular disease [cite: 1326]
* Adjust dose with other CNS depressants; carefully observe for pigmentary retinopathy and lenticular pigmentation [cite: 1327]
* Blood dyscrasias and liver damage reported with related drugs [cite: 1328]
* Prolactin elevation: may elevate prolactin levels persisting during chronic administration. Clinical significance is unknown for most patients. [cite: 1329, 1332]
* Leukopenia, neutropenia, and agranulocytosis: Monitor CBC frequently during first few months in patients with risk factors. [cite: 1339, 1340, 1341, 1342]
* Tardive dyskinesia: Potentially irreversible, involuntary, dyskinetic movements may develop. Prescribe to minimize occurrence; use smallest dose and shortest duration. [cite: 1343, 1344, 1347, 1352, 1354]
* Neuroleptic malignant syndrome (NMS): Potentially fatal symptom complex; immediate discontinuation of antipsychotic drugs is required. [cite: 1362, 1363, 1368]
