other required inputs known; gate false is `SchizophreniaEstablished No`
(first-time case) with no unknown/missing/conflict among required inputs. Gate
false is `not_applicable` with no posterior claim, never a negative finding or
a fake negative clozapine posterior; unknown/missing/conflict — including
missing trial-adequacy or observation-window context — pauses as
`needs_clarification` rather than coercing to a known value and is never
coerced to `No`. A missing trial assessment is never treated as an
adequate-trial finding. `UrgentSafetyConcern Present` renders a parallel urgent
banner from saved structured inputs independently of estimation latency —
never a computed risk percentage. This R7 registration package keeps a
distinct network identity from the separate F5 no-improvement follow-up even
though BN-07 concepts overlap. No dose, route, frequency or active/stopped
fields are collected or accepted. Whether review outputs behave as
deterministic rules or estimated tables is an open assumption recorded in the
dossier; this draft uses estimated-table semantics without approval, as are
trial-adequacy/observation-window operationalization and monitoring/willingness
input meanings. The candidate 15-field history extends `history.v1` in
isolation; runtime history, projections and readiness remain unchanged.
