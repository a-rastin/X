Why this shape: gate true requires episode `Active` (`Resolved` executes
post-resolution review) with all other required inputs known —
onset/medication-timeline/association/alternative-cause/differential/safety/
preference context; gate false is `NoEpisode` (absent case) with no
unknown/missing/conflict among required inputs. Gate false is
`not_applicable` with no posterior claim, never a negative finding or a fake
negative review posterior; unknown/missing/conflict — including
`not_assessed`, missing onset/medication-timeline/association/
alternative-cause/differential/safety/preference context — pauses as
`needs_clarification` rather than coercing to a known value and is never
coerced to `NoEpisode`. Dx-criteria completion is required only when the
episode is present; there is no total score and no etiology is inferred from
an unreviewed total. Severity is an independent clinician-reviewed category
(`mild|moderate|severe`), never derived from a score band. Airway compromise,
laryngeal involvement, respiratory distress or other acute safety concern
requires urgent evaluation rather than attribution to uncomplicated dystonia:
the urgent airway banner renders from saved structured inputs in parallel,
independently of estimation latency — never a computed risk percentage — and
the 8th candidate history field gates applicability and drives the banner
with no separate urgent network node. This F4 follow-up package keeps a
distinct network identity for acute-dystonia review. No dose, route, frequency
or active/stopped fields are collected or accepted. Whether review outputs
behave as deterministic rules or estimated tables is an open assumption
recorded in the dossier; this draft uses estimated-table semantics without
approval, as are dx-criteria complete-source/item/completeness/severity/
network definitions, severity operationalization and airway-triage input
meanings. STATEMENT-11 Quality Measurement Consideration is recorded (not
appropriate for quality measure/decision support). The candidate 8-field
history extends `history.v1` in isolation; runtime history, projections and
readiness remain unchanged.
