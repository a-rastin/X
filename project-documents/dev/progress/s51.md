## Scope and synthetic basis

- §1 admin-only archive/unarchive, no permanent deletion. Under the
  explicitly provisional archive policy: blocked create/edit/sign on
  archived records, retained private read-only drafts, resumable by author
  after unarchive. This policy is NOT owner-confirmed.
- §2 deactivate with retained drafts revokes access and queued work;
  confirmed discard applies only to the reviewed draft-set revision.
  Changing that set invalidates confirmation; reactivation does not
  resurrect discarded drafts.
- §3 simultaneous draft creates, sign/save, sign/slider/reset,
  sign/demographic edit, and sign/deactivation have one valid serial
  outcome. Archive races tested under the declared provisional policy. No
  second draft or stale signature is possible.
- §4 relevant demographic edits mark affected results/adjustments stale
  without granting draft access; notes do not rerun inference or
  invalidate probability acceptance. Retained deactivated-author drafts
  occupy the slot until reactivation or confirmed discard;
  signed/discarded drafts release it.
