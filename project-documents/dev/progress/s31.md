Why this shape: gate true requires `SchizophreniaEstablished Yes` plus
`SubstantialAggressionRisk Yes` plus
`AggressionRiskPersistsDespiteOtherTreatments Yes` with all other required
inputs known; gate false is any of those three explicitly `No` with no
unknown/missing/conflict among required inputs. Gate false is
`not_applicable` with no posterior claim, never a negative finding;
unknown/missing/conflict pauses as `needs_clarification` rather than coercing
to a known value and is never coerced to `No`. A PANSS hostility item score
alone never maps the gate; clinician synthesis with source periods and
clinician-reported fields is required. `UrgentSafetyConcern Present` renders a
parallel urgent banner from saved structured inputs independently of
estimation latency — never a computed risk percentage. Whether review outputs
behave as deterministic rules or estimated tables is an open assumption
recorded in the dossier; this draft uses estimated-table semantics without
approval. The candidate 10-field history extends `history.v1` in isolation;
runtime history, projections and readiness remain unchanged.
