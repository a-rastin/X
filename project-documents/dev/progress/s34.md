Why this shape: gate true requires `AkathisiaAssessment Present` with
all 12 other required inputs known; gate false is
`AkathisiaAssessment Absent` (absent case) with no
unknown/missing/conflict among required inputs. Gate false is
`not_applicable` with no posterior claim, never a negative finding or a fake
negative review posterior; unknown/missing/conflict — including Unresolved,
unknown, not_assessed, missing alternative-cause/exposure/onset/
differential/distress/safety/preference context — pauses as
`needs_clarification` rather than coercing to a known value and is never
coerced to `Absent`. A missing BARS item assessment never becomes a negative
finding and no diagnosis is inferred from an unreviewed BARS total; the global
2-or-greater threshold is a review candidate for presence only, never an
activation claim. Motor restlessness and distress are clinician-reviewed
categories without invented bands and with no summed severity rule.
Objective movements without subjective restlessness are pseudoakathisia
(reassess, never assume). `UrgentSafetyConcern Present` renders a parallel
urgent banner from saved structured inputs independently of estimation
latency — never a computed risk percentage. This F2 follow-up package keeps a
distinct network identity for akathisia review. No dose, route, frequency or
active/stopped fields are collected or accepted. Whether review outputs behave
as deterministic rules or estimated tables is an open assumption recorded in
the dossier; this draft uses estimated-table semantics without approval, as are
BARS complete-source/item/completeness/severity/network definitions, severity
operationalization and benzodiazepine/propranolol safety input meanings. The
candidate 13-field history extends `history.v1` in isolation; runtime history,
projections and readiness remain unchanged.
