Why this shape: gate true requires `ParkinsonismAssessment Present` with
all 13 other required inputs known; gate false is
`ParkinsonismAssessment Absent` (absent case) with no
unknown/missing/conflict among required inputs. Gate false is
`not_applicable` with no posterior claim, never a negative finding or a fake
negative review posterior; unknown/missing/conflict — including Unresolved,
unknown, not_assessed, missing onset/medication-timeline/alternative-cause/
tardive-differential/NMS-triage/safety/preference context — pauses as
`needs_clarification` rather than coercing to a known value and is never
coerced to `Absent`. A missing SAS item assessment never becomes a negative
finding and no etiology is inferred from an unreviewed SAS total or mean; the
original up-to-0.3 normal-range convention and the later 0.7 screening
proposal are review candidates only and no universal mild/moderate/severe
bands are created from historical thresholds. Severity is an independent
clinician-reviewed category, never derived from SAS bands. Fever, altered
consciousness, autonomic instability or severe generalized rigidity requires
urgent NMS evaluation rather than attribution to uncomplicated parkinsonism:
the urgent banner renders from saved structured inputs in parallel,
independently of estimation latency — never a computed risk percentage — and
BN-12 carries no urgent network node by design, so the 14th candidate history
field gates applicability and drives the banner with no node mapping. This F3
follow-up package keeps a distinct network identity for parkinsonism review.
No dose, route, frequency or active/stopped fields are collected or accepted.
Whether review outputs behave as deterministic rules or estimated tables is an
open assumption recorded in the dossier; this draft uses estimated-table
semantics without approval, as are SAS complete-source/item/completeness/
severity/network definitions, severity operationalization and NMS-triage input
meanings. The candidate 14-field history extends `history.v1` in isolation;
runtime history, projections and readiness remain unchanged.
