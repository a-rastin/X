# Checks and constraints that can block vibecoding

Reviewed 2026-10-04. Scope: every file under `project-documents/dev` and `project-documents/bayesian-networks` (8 nonempty Markdown documents, an empty progress tracker, 11 network XML files, the XSD, and its Python test).

This is an audit of the supplied specifications, not a claim that an application already enforces these checks. Your stated objective is an experimental research app without permission or safety approval friction. The documents still prescribe substantial approval and workflow gates. This report identifies them; it does not remove or change them.

References below use repository-relative paths and source line numbers. Repeated requirements are grouped by behavior. “Product rule” means stated in the requirements or labeled confirmed in the design; “proposal” means a proposed implementation or unresolved policy. A documented rule can conflict with your current preference without becoming a technical necessity.

## Most consequential blockers

1. **Human approval before releasing clinical content.** Assessment definitions, history/severity fields, DDI data, all thirteen question packages, and workflow bundles need owner review. An agent may draft them but cannot approve them. These gates explicitly block real-content release and some form implementation; synthetic engineering can continue. See `tasks.md` S08–S12, S18, S26–S39 and S59–S62.
2. **All supplied networks are incomplete.** Every BN passes the supplied XSD, but each lacks definitions for some nature nodes. Clearing `inference_allowed=false` does not supply missing tables, complete graph contracts, prompts, mappings or templates.
3. **No manual signing fallback.** A failed original question stops later questions and prevents signing, even if a physician writes a complete plan manually. This is a product policy, not a mathematical prerequisite for saving a research plan.
4. **Private author-owned drafts and one draft per patient.** Other physicians and ordinary administrator routes cannot access the draft. A retained draft belonging to a deactivated author still blocks new encounters until reactivation or confirmed discard.
5. **Exact current-result acceptance.** Every applicable question needs explicit acceptance, including unchanged originals. Pending/failed/stale calculations or later relevant patient edits block signing.
6. **Provider/MCP controls.** Destination allowlists, isolated authorization grants, one read-only tool, strict output validation, retries, queue admission and time/resource limits can reject calls or stop generation.
7. **Specification conflicts.** The BNs describe fixed clinician-review mappings and evidence-oriented inputs, while the app estimates every CPT and executes with empty evidence. Several XML files also record source restrictions against electronic decision support. These require specification choices, not simply disabling checks.

## 5. Safety constraints inside the supplied Bayesian networks

### 5.1 Advisory flags versus executable defects

The XML `PROPERTY` entries are arbitrary strings. The XSD does not enforce their clinical meaning, presence or freshness (`schema.xml:80–100`). Therefore `inference_enabled=false`, `inference_allowed=false`, `deployment_allowed=false`, `deployment_gate`, `execution_contract`, `runtime_warning` and `validation_status` are **documented intent**, not executable locks. A consumer may enforce them; no application enforcement was established by this review.

All eleven XML files validated against the supplied XSD during this audit. Counting each variable without a matching `DEFINITION/FOR` produced the following structural inventory. All these variables are nature nodes, so missing definitions prevent complete standard BN execution as supplied.

| `BN-05.xml` | 20 | 6 | 14 | Lines 16–23: structural-only/incomplete; inference disabled; consumer must reject incomplete definitions |

### 5.2 Shared clinical constraints and what they actually block

- **No invented priors/default uniform probabilities.** Parameter policies intentionally omit unsupported distributions. This conflicts with an unrestricted “just make it executable” shortcut. Complete run-local LLM estimates are allowed by the app design, but the released reference-network and semantic contract still need to be defined.
- **Missing is not negative.** Network-level and repeated variable-level missingness properties require unobserved inputs rather than No/Absent/Resolved/NotEstablished; some networks prohibit partial-evidence inference and derived-output display with required inputs missing. The application converts required missing inputs into clarification rather than guessing.
- **Dated clinician-verified facts and contradiction reconciliation.** Exposure, syndrome attribution, risk, treatment history, medical review, consent/preference and practical readiness are external assessments. Derived review outputs must not be reentered as independent evidence. These are content/workflow requirements, not automatically executable XML checks.
- **Review outputs are not autonomous treatment.** The networks exclude automatic prescribing, dose/route/duration/taper/restart instructions, stopping/withholding, forced treatment, treatment rankings, causal treatment-effect claims, risk predictions and expected-utility optimization in various combinations. These restrict what templates may claim; they do not inherently prevent displaying a research calculation.
- **Urgent concerns take priority; other concerns remain visible.** Priority outputs must not hide parallel review needs, delay urgent care or imply clearance when routine. These are presentation/clinical-scope constraints, not a general application “stop all work” switch.
- **Provisional independence and measurement assumptions.** Root dependencies and measurement error require review before adding priors. A normalized joint distribution can still express unreviewed assumptions. The review gate is epistemic/governance; shape/normalization checks are mathematical.
- **No obsolete regulatory or source-category conversion shortcuts.** XML notes exclude source-derived dosing rules, obsolete enrollment assumptions and interpretation of ordinal adverse-effect categories or guideline evidence grades as patient probabilities. This audit reports those repository notes without independently checking current medical/regulatory guidance.

## 7. Things that are not hard safety blockers here

- The login research notice is required display text; no separate login acknowledgment is specified.
- Diagnosis bypass is allowed without a reason. Below-threshold diagnosis is not an absolute treatment-generation prohibition.
- PANSS/C-SSRS can be skipped. Only declared required question inputs subsequently cause clarification.
- Catalog drugs without DDI coverage remain selectable. DDI severity alone does not authorize a new signing hard stop.
- There is no password-complexity policy, mandatory initial password-change gate or application session timeout.
- No synthetic-only patient policy or production PHI/compliance certification gate is imposed by the baseline. Synthetic data requirements apply to specific tests/capability checks.
- A probability edit need not change the recommendation. That is valid behavior, not a failed recalculation.
- Local slider recalculation/reset/retry requires no LLM/MCP/provider permission or availability once a current successful baseline exists.
- Missing provider/content disables new generation, not all chart/admin work. Completed current baselines remain usable.
- XML safety/validation properties do not themselves execute checks. A consumer must implement enforcement.
- `progress-tracker.md` is empty and introduces no checks.

## 8. Practical distinction for this research app

The direct candidates for removing permission friction are **human content-release approvals, author/role restrictions, single-draft policy, confirmations, mandatory probability acceptance, the no-manual-signing policy, provisional archive locks, and process/release gates**. They are choices about who may proceed and when.

The technical checks have different consequences: **complete normalized CPTs, known graph/state ordering, matching result revisions, conflict-safe persistence, coherent snapshots and valid restore artifacts** define whether the result corresponds to the saved inputs and whether data remain consistent. Exact six-decimal precision, all-or-nothing sequential processing, no approximate inference, queue sizes/timeouts and mandatory real MCP are configurable design choices surrounding those invariants. Security controls such as endpoint allowlists, CSRF, parser isolation and secret handling add setup/rejection behavior but do not establish clinical validity.

Removing every gate indiscriminately would not resolve the missing packages, incomplete networks or contradictory modeling semantics. For uninterrupted vibecoding, the specifications need an explicit experimental workflow plus complete executable data contracts; the currently documented synthetic-fixture path already allows substantial engineering without waiting for clinical approval.
